About Me

Every day, I am a product designer who cares about both appearance and functionality. I design digital experiences that are simple and easy to understand, combining logic and personality in just the right way.

Right now, I am in Lagos, Nigeria, and I've worked on products in fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, and security—partnering with developers, PMs, and everyday users to turn ideas into reality.

Outside of work, you'll probably find me watching movies, making TikTok videos, or reading a book.
