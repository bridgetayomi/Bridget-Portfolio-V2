Case Study • Fintech & Utility Bill Payments

My Billa
Fintech & Bills

Billa is fintech platform that makes everyday payments easier. Users can buy airtime and data, pay utility bills, and shop for digital products, all in one place.

Role Product Designer (Fintech UX)
Focus Fintech, Bills & Transactions
Duration 8 Weeks
Platform Web & Mobile
Overview

Making Everyday Payments Easier

Paying for everyday essentials like airtime, data, electricity, water, and cable TV can mean switching between different platforms and payment methods. This can make simple transactions feel unnecessarily complicated.
My Billa was designed to bring these everyday payments into one platform. Users can purchase airtime and data, pay utility bills, and manage multiple transactions through a single checkout experience.
A key part of the experience is the Billa Bucket which allows users to add different bill payments to one place before completing checkout.

The platform also includes a digital marketplace where creators and merchants can sell digital products such as ebooks, templates, and tickets, bringing everyday payments and digital purchases together in one ecosystem..

The Primary Goal

Make Everyday Payments Simpler and More Convenient

The goal was to create a fintech platform that makes it easier for users to manage everyday payments in one place. Instead of completing airtime, data, and utility payments separately across different platforms, Billa brings these transactions into a connected experience. The Billa Bucket allows users to add multiple payments before checking out, reducing the need to repeat the same process for every transaction. The aim was to make bill payments easier to understand, organise, and complete.

The Challenge

Managing Multiple Payments Can Be Tedious

Everyday payments such as airtime, data, electricity, and cable TV are often handled as separate transactions. When users need to make several payments, they may have to repeat the same steps for each one. Billa needed a simpler way to help users manage multiple payments in one place, while making it easy to review their selections, choose a payment method, and complete checkout without unnecessary steps.

The Fintech Solution

The Billa Bucket

I designed the Billa Bucket as a central place where users can bring together multiple purchases and payments before checkout. Instead of completing each transaction separately, users can add airtime, data, utility bills, and digital products to their bucket and review everything in one place. The experience was designed to make it easier to manage multiple transactions, understand what you're paying for, and complete checkout through a single, organised flow.

The Process

Design & Discovery

1. User Research & Transaction Anxiety

DISCOVERY

I conducted research to understand the friction points users face during high-frequency financial transactions. The study revealed three critical friction patterns:

74%

Frustrated by paying multiple gateway convenience fees when recharging airtime and utilities individually.

68%

Experienced transaction anxiety from delayed electricity token delivery and lack of immediate confirmation.

82%

Recharge airtime & data for multiple SIMs, family members, or business staff in single monthly sessions.

Early stages of fintech user research and problem synthesis

2. User Personas

EMPATHY

Synthesized from our quantitative data, two personas guided the UX: our primary high-frequency bill payer needing speed and accuracy, and our secondary creator merchant utilizing the storefront.

Primary Persona: High-Frequency Bill Payer

Needs instant multi-network airtime top-ups, high-volume data plans for household & staff, verified electricity meter tokens, and a single consolidated checkout receipt.

Secondary Persona: Digital Creator & Merchant

Wants a hassle-free storefront to sell digital downloads (ebooks, guides, tickets) and automatically use earned balances to settle everyday utilities.

Results & Impact

Fintech Validation & Real-World Impact

After deploying the redesigned bills payment flows, I led live usability testing sessions with both individual power users and SME operations managers. The testing confirmed that bundling multi-bill payments through the Billa Bucket yielded profound efficiencies:

60% Reduction in time-to-checkout for multi-service payments
99.4% Carrier & meter validation accuracy prior to debit
Multi-Org Adopted by companies for team airtime, data & power bills
Key Takeaway

Personal Reflection

“Designing Billa reinforced that fintech excellence isn't just about moving money—it's about stripping away cognitive friction from repetitive daily obligations. Transforming airtime, data, and utility payments from fragmented chores into an instantaneous bundled checkout established a dependable transaction foundation that users rely on daily.”

Deep Dive & Design Decisions

Unlike traditional fintech portals where bill payments, airtime recharges, and digital product sales require independent checkout flows, Billa Bucket aggregates disparate service APIs into a single cart checkout, cutting user payment steps and gateway fees by over 60%.

I implemented live meter account lookup and carrier prefix auto-detection directly into the form fields. The UI verifies the subscriber or meter name against the utility provider's database prior to adding the bill to the bucket, virtually eliminating mistyped account transactions.

The storefront is a secondary capability that allows creators and merchants to sell digital goods such as ebooks, guides, and event tickets. Revenues generated from the storefront flow directly into the user's Billa wallet, creating a closed loop where earnings can immediately be used to settle utility bills without external withdrawal hurdles.
