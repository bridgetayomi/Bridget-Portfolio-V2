Orgsphere
HR Platform

Orgsphere is a platform designed to simplify request approvals, performance reviews, employee requests (leave, expense, training), and employee records by bringing them into one cohesive dashboard.

Role Product Designer
Team 2 PMs, 1 Developer
Duration 12 Weeks
Platform Web & Mobile
Overview

One Platform for Every HR Workflow

Orgsphere is a platform built to integrate legacy systems, payrolls, request approvals, performance review management, and employee records in one place. It helps HR managers handle employee requests efficiently — covering leave applications, expense approvals, appraisals, announcements, and training management — all under one unified system.

The Primary Goal

Build a Digital Workspace That Works for Everyone

The goal was to create a platform that tracks and manages employee requests seamlessly, standardises the multi-tier request approval flow, keeps records of employee performance and appraisals, and provides a comprehensive set of management tools for the HR team.

The Problem

Paper-Based Workflows Slowing Everything Down

Staff members could not easily make requests, verify their statuses, or get fast approvals. Managers struggled to track their team's requests. Most processes were handled manually — paper forms led to data security risks, slow processing times, and missing documentation.

The Solution

A Unified Request & Approval Hub

I designed an intuitive, responsive web and mobile application for employees, managers, and HR teams. The platform standardises approval workflows to increase corporate transparency and automatically channels financial requests to the appropriate departments for validation.

2. User Personas

EMPATHY

I created key user personas that reflect the needs, goals, and frustrations of users. These personas helped guide design decisions and kept the focus on solving the right problems.

Employee

Wants a simple, transparent way to file requests (leave, discounts, expense reimbursement) and track progress in real time.

Line Manager

Needs a clear dashboard to review pending requests, compare team availability, and approve or reject submissions quickly.

HR Team

Requires an automated portal to oversee compliance, manage employee profiles, track approvals, and manage performance logs.

6. Outcome

RESULT

The Orgsphere platform streamlined key HR processes by digitising request approvals, performance management, and employee records. Replacing paper-based workflows with a centralised platform improved communication, reduced processing time, and gave both employees and HR teams better visibility into everyday operations.

7. Reflection

LEARNINGS

Working on this project gave me valuable experience designing a large-scale HR system with multiple user roles and interconnected workflows. It pushed me to think beyond individual screens and focus on how different parts of the product fit together to create a consistent user experience. As the platform continues to evolve, additional features — including payroll management — are planned to support more of the organisation's HR operations.
