Overview

Africa's Central Pension Remittance Switch

PenCentral (formerly PensionCentral) is a pension remittance switch infrastructure for Africa. The platform helps organisations of all kinds — from startups and FinTechs to legacy banks and large corporates — streamline and automate their pension payment workflows, centrally remitting payments to desired PFAs and PFCs in seconds.

My design work has contributed to platforms serving 10,000+ users and ₦300B+ in transaction value, including a pension platform redesign that improved user engagement by 30% and onboarding workflows that reduced customer sign-up errors by 25%.

The Problem

A Broken Remittance Process

  • Multiple paperwork processes slowing down each payment cycle
  • Enormous time investment required to avoid data entry errors
  • Long processing and crediting times spanning 3 weeks to 1 month
  • Large amounts of uncredited funds sitting with PFCs
  • No mechanism to detect when an employee migrates to another PFA
The Goal

Seconds, Not Weeks

Design a central remittance switch that cuts payment times to seconds, automates RSA validation, integrates payment gateway redundancy, and simplifies employer/employee contribution splitting.

The Solution

One Dashboard, All PFAs

A robust switch dashboard enabling organisations to centrally manage all pension payments — one upload, one payment, instantly distributed to all respective PFAs.

Design Decisions

Organisations deal with employees spread across multiple PFAs. A switch model means one schedule upload and one payment gets automatically routed to every PFA — removing the need for HR teams to maintain separate relationships, logins, and workflows with each administrator.

Before any payment is processed, PenCentral pings PENCOM's registry to confirm the employee's active RSA PIN and current PFA. If an employee has switched PFAs, the system routes the contribution correctly — preventing funds from landing in the wrong account and sitting uncredited.
