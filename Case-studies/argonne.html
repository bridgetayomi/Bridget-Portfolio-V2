Overview

The Complete Shopping Journey

Argon is an e-commerce marketplace that allows customers to discover products from different stores, compare products, review sellers and complete purchases through multiple payment options. I worked on the customer experience across the shopping journey, from discovering a product to completing checkout and tracking an order after purchase.

The experience includes product discovery, store pages, product comparison, reviews and ratings, cart, checkout, bank transfer, KoraPay, Buy Now Pay Later, order tracking and email notifications.

The Challenge

Navigating Choice & Building Trust

The marketplace needed a shopping experience that could help customers confidently move from discovering a product to completing a purchase. Customers needed to be able to:

  • Understand the product and evaluate the seller.
  • Compare multiple options before committing.
  • Choose how they wanted to pay (BNPL, Bank Transfer, Card).
  • Know exactly what was happening with their order post-purchase.
The Goal

A Confident Marketplace

Design a complete marketplace experience that makes it easy for customers to discover products, evaluate sellers, read reviews, use multiple payment methods (including BNPL), and accurately track their orders.

The Solution

End-to-End Shopping

A structured journey connecting:

  • • Discovery & Smart Comparisons
  • • Transparent Store & Review Pages
  • • Flexible Payment (KoraPay, BNPL)
  • • Real-time Post-Purchase Tracking

The Process

Setting the Foundation

User Journey & Info Architecture

I mapped the customer's journey from discovering a product to receiving the order: Discover → Explore → Evaluate → Compare → Cart → Checkout → Payment → Confirmation → Tracking → Delivery. I then structured the main areas of the marketplace including Home, Product Details, Shopping, Orders, and Account configurations.

User Flow

The main shopping flow starts with product discovery and ends with order delivery, ensuring customers seamlessly transition from viewing a product, evaluating the seller, comparing options, and securely checking out with their preferred payment method.

Interface Design

High Fidelity Designs

Instead of showing every screen individually, I have grouped the interfaces by journey to illustrate how the user effortlessly moves through the Argon marketplace ecosystem.

Store Experience & Comparison

The checkout flow keeps the customer aware of exactly what they are paying. The BNPL experience is a separate, dedicated flow allowing customers to select plans, view installment breakdowns, and manage verifications.

The experience continues after checkout. Customers can access their orders, see current status (Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered), and receive matching email notifications at every touchpoint.

Design System & Edge Cases

A strong e-commerce experience cannot only account for the successful purchase. I designed reusable components and accounted for edge cases (Out of Stock, Payment Failed, BNPL Declined, Empty Cart).

Results & Impact

The Final Experience

Argon brings together the full e-commerce journey in one marketplace experience. The result is a complete ecosystem designed around the customer's journey from discovery to delivery.

What I Learned

Designing Argon helped me think about e-commerce as a complete journey rather than a collection of individual screens. The customer experience starts before the purchase and continues after payment.

That meant thinking about how customers discover products, evaluate sellers, compare items, and make payment decisions. It also reinforced the importance of designing different states (like BNPL failures or shipping updates) and not only the ideal successful journey.
