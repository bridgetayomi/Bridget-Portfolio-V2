HEALTHCARE

A healthtech platform designed to connect companies, HMOs, employees, hospitals, and wellness providers in one unified ecosystem.

RoleProduct Designer
Duration8 Weeks

PlatformWeb
Overview

Connecting Companies, HMOs, Employees and Healthcare Providers

HealthFusion had to support HR managers, HMOs, and employees, each with different roles, workflows, and information needs. The challenge was designing a system that could handle these differences while keeping the experience clear, consistent, and easy to navigate. HealthFusion brings different parts of the healthcare and insurance ecosystem into one platform. I joined the project after the initial website had already been created.
My work focused on designing the insurance and wellness experiences, including product flows and interfaces for HR teams, HMOs, employees, and other users across the platform.

I worked directly with the Product Owner and collaborated with stakeholders and developers from the early product requirements through design, implementation, and testing.

The Product Challenge

Multiple users. Different needs. One ecosystem.

HealthFusion serves a healthcare ecosystem made up of HMOs, HR managers, employees, and hospitals, each with different goals and workflows. The challenge was creating experiences that felt clear and connected while handling multiple roles, complex information, and large amounts of data.

The Primary Goal

Simplicity at Scale

Make a complex healthcare platform easier to navigate and manage.
Create clear user experiences for each role while keeping the overall ecosystem visually and functionally consistent.

The Solution

Role-Based Architecture

designed the product around the different roles using it. I mapped out their main workflows, simplified complicated processes, and created reusable components to keep the experience consistent across the platform.

The Process

Setting the Foundation

01. Understanding the Product

I started by understanding the existing product, the different users and how the different parts of the ecosystem connected. I reviewed requirements with the Product Owner to identify key journeys that needed improvement.

02. Mapping the Experience

I mapped the major journeys and information structures before moving into high fidelity design. This helped define how different users would move through the platform and how information should be presented at each stage.

Results & Impact

A Unified Healthcare Ecosystem

The project resulted in a structured set of experiences across HealthFusion's insurance, wellness and healthcare management ecosystem. I designed interfaces for different user roles while creating consistent patterns across dashboards, forms, tables, workflows and management tools.

What I Learned

HealthFusion taught me how to approach product design beyond individual screens. The product required me to think about systems, user roles, business requirements, data, workflows and implementation at the same time. Working directly with the Product Owner and developers also helped me become more involved throughout the product lifecycle, from understanding requirements to reviewing the final implementation.

Deep Dive

Behind the Scenes

I broke the product down by user role and workflow instead of trying to design the entire ecosystem at once. This made it easier to understand what each user needed and how their actions connected with other parts of the platform.

I used clear hierarchy, structured tables, filters, cards, status indicators, progressive disclosure and guided flows to make large amounts of information easier to scan and manage.

I worked with developers throughout implementation rather than treating handoff as the end of the design process. I participated in design reviews, clarified interactions, reviewed implemented screens and followed up on outstanding design issues.

My role went well beyond designing interfaces. While the product was being developed, I stayed in close contact with the engineering team, led design review meetings, and set up a dedicated sheet to track sprints and ensure full alignment. After the build was finished, I was given test scripts and ran thorough end-to-end product testing before launch, in addition to defining the main user flows, information architecture, and stakeholder discussions.
